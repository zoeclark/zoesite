rooms

F.A.Q

These four galleries present digital works created with open-source tools and the platform Midjourney. Midjourney is a diffusion-based model: it generates images by gradually refining random noise into patterns guided by text prompts.

The 'rooms' were made in chronological order in the summer of 2025. Moving in sequence — Gallery 1 first, then 2, 3, and 4 — each room is more complex than the last.

No copyrighted material was used in their creation. They are dedicated to all the curators whose work made it possible for me to access such rich reference material for this art.

Some digital collections used include:

  • Wellcome Collection
  • The Book of Kells Experience (Trinity College Dublin)
  • Georgia O’Keeffe Museum
  • New York Public Library Digital Collections
